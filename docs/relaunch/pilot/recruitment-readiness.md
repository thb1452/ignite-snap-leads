# Pilot recruitment readiness

**No approved recipient set, invitations, participants or observed sessions.** Candidate and correspondence evidence remains private. This public document contains no identities, account metadata, recruitment counts or individual histories.

A waitlist entry, former purchase, feature-specific signup or staff relationship does not establish current market fit or participation consent. Current form wording cannot by itself establish what a historical visitor agreed to. Resolve ambiguous identities and preferred addresses before proposing an invitation; do not send to multiple possible addresses to test which belongs to a person.

## Qualification before admission

Review exact recipients and invitation text before any outreach. An approved, individually addressed invitation should ask whether the person wants to participate and request only:

1. Their active city/market, compared with actually accepted coverage.
2. Whether they personally evaluated or followed up a property recently and their role in that work.
3. Their normal device.
4. Availability for an initial 25-minute session and a five-minute return visit on day 3–7.
5. Explicit participation agreement; recording agreement is separate and optional.

A decline ends recruitment. Do not repurpose a feature-specific notification list as a general contact list. Additional permissioned referrals require identity verification and the same eligibility/consent review.

Use [internal-rehearsal.md](internal-rehearsal.md) for a clearly labeled synthetic exercise while recruitment and release gates remain open. That exercise cannot establish buyer completion, willingness to pay, return use or retention. Keep the customer [scorecard.json](scorecard.json) unobserved until real consent and sessions exist. Day 3–7 return behavior requires elapsed days and cannot be completed during one implementation session.
